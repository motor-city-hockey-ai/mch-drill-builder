HOCKEY CAMPS

Camp Planner

Everything is full-width and stacked. Open or close any section.

Staff StandardsRequired for every camp

Arrival — 8:30 AM sharp. Be in the locker room ready to work. Help players get dressed and greet / communicate with every parent who attends.

Off-Ice. Stay active with the players: coach, guide and supervise. Do not stand around doing nothing.

Lunch. No cell phone use. Sit with the kids, communicate with them and stay engaged.

Before the second ice hour. Help players get dressed and fully prepared for the ice.

Locker-room supervision. Players are never allowed in a locker room without a coach. If a coach leaves, another coach must take over before that coach exits. Supervision must be continuous.

Post-camp. Greet parents, give every player a high five and thank parents for coming when you are in the locker room.
